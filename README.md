# European Logistics Radar

**Live:** https://suvashgiri.github.io/european-logistics-radar/

Independent student project by Suvash Chantin-Giri, built with an AI assistant.
Not affiliated with or endorsed by EQT. Public sources only.

## What it does

- Ranks 13 European logistics markets (UK, Netherlands, Germany, Poland, Spain, the Nordics) out of 100 on five signals: low vacancy, rising rents, limited new supply, strong demand and recent deal activity.
- Maps EQT Real Estate's publicly announced European logistics deals against those markets, to show where the record is established, where it is a toe-hold, and where no site was found.
- Ends with a one-page investment committee style memo: *The Nordic gap: why Denmark first, and when Norway*.

## Method

Every figure is taken from public broker research and reputable news, with its source and date beside it. Where a figure was not found, the signal scores half its points and is labelled "not found". The supply signal is a judgement from broker wording and is labelled as an estimate. Full method and limits are on the page.

## Limits

Brokers define vacancy, take-up and prime rent differently, and periods differ. Off-market and undisclosed deals are invisible. It is a snapshot from October 2026 and does not update on its own.
