        Limits

        • Brokers measure differently. Colliers, Savills, CBRE, JLL, Cushman & Wakefield, BNP Paribas, Knight Frank and Catella define vacancy, take-up and prime rent in their own ways. Mixing them is a rough comparison, not a clean one.
        • Periods differ. Some figures are from early in the year, others from the third quarter. The date is beside every number.
        • Many full reports are paywalled. Where only a summary was public, missing figures are marked not found.
        • Deal counts reflect what public news reported and what this search found. Off-market and undisclosed deals are invisible.
        • The supply score is a judgement from broker wording, labelled as an estimate.
        • EQT's map shows only what EQT or reputable news announced. EQT's full European portfolio, disposals and pipeline are not public in this detail.
        • Finland, France, Italy, Belgium, Austria and Czechia are outside the radar, though EQT owns assets in some of them. Norway is scored for the Oslo region only. Vestby, Berger, Gardermoen and Drammen have no separate public figures.
        • It cannot see rent free periods, tenant credit, building quality, land prices or planning risk. These decide a real deal.
        • It is a snapshot. It does not update on its own.
